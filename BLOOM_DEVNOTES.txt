THE IDEA (Todd)
---------------
A calm space adventure inspired by Life Bubble. An astronaut is stranded on
a dead little planet. A glowing bubble keeps them alive; growing the bubble
brings the planet back to life (cracked red earth turns to grass, the lava
river runs blue, bare trees turn green). Go places, gather, collect, earn
better tools, and get better at things over time. No enemies, no losing.

Todd's long-term design
- Planets after the first come from a random generator (same screen,
  structures and obstacles chosen at random) instead of hand-made layouts.
- Each planet uses 3-6 materials from a long master list that grows.
- One or two materials upgrade a machine or rebuild a broken pre-placed
  item (like the bridge). Find and collect enough to restore the planet.
- Bubble generators upgrade a few times; sometimes a bubble must be maxed
  out to connect to the next area. Maxed equipment shows MAX instead of costs.
- Oxygen: an air timer runs outside the bubbles (rescue astronauts, mine
  just outside, cross dead ground as a shortcut). Suit upgrades add time.
- Easter eggs (later): an obscure spot that, mined or cleared enough,
  reveals a small cave / room / old building / old ship with special
  non-critical materials that make the planet faster or cheaper to finish.
- Carry-over item (later): one special item taken to the next world and
  combined there with a few local resources makes a tool 3-5 levels better.
- Level up: skills grow with use; tool upgrades; collection album with set
  rewards (later); rescued astronauts as helpers; upgrade machines in play.

PHASE 1 (built)
---------------
Engine and look
- three.js, smooth rounded toy-like shapes, bright colours, close angled
  camera, press-and-pull joystick (and arrow keys / WASD on a laptop).
- The planet is a floating disc (radius 62) over a starfield.
- Ground shader: cracked red earth (Voronoi cracks) outside bubbles, grass
  inside, a cyan glow at every bubble edge. Up to 12 bubbles.
- River ribbon: lava outside a bubble, flowing water inside.
- Grass tufts and flowers grow only inside bubbles; pebbles only outside.
  Trees switch between bare/dead and green as the bubble reaches them.
- Bubble domes: soft cyan fresnel shells that grow smoothly.
- Auto quality: lower resolution, then no shadows, if the phone struggles.

Planet 1 "Ember" (src/planet.js is a description; a generator will
produce the same shape later)
- Materials: wood (trees), stone (boulders), red crystal, gold (ore; needs
  the crystal pickaxe).
- 7 bubble generators (home is built; 6 build sites with signposts),
  each with 2-4 levels and per-level costs, MAX badge when done.
- Broken bridge across the river (repair to reach the north).
- 2 extractors (red crystal, gold): build, then they mine on their own into
  storage; walk past to collect; upgrade for speed/storage.
- Workbench (inside the starting bubble): pickaxe, axe, backpack, suit.
- 4 lost astronauts in HELP bubbles; walk up and lead them into any bubble.
  Each rescue: +5 bag space; they wait by the workbench and wave.
- Air: 12s outside at first (20/30/45 with suit upgrades). At zero, the
  suit floats you gently back into the nearest bubble; nothing is lost.
- Skills: mining and woodcutting levels rise with use (5% faster swings per
  level, and a growing chance of a bonus piece).
- Goal: 50% of the planet alive, then repair the rocket -> lift off.
  (All generators maxed gives about 57%.)
- Autosave every 5s and when the app is hidden; Continue / Start over.

Playtest (automated): gathering, generator upgrade, building outside with
air, float home, bridge repair, rescue, workbench upgrade, extractor, rocket
and lift-off all worked; no errors.

PHASE 2 (Nov 3) — from Todd's notes
-----------------------------------
Decisions with Todd: no storage locker yet (try touch-gathering first);
refining machines work load -> wait -> collect.

Fixes
- Gathering now needs touch: you only work on a tree/rock when you're right
  up against it (REACH 0.75 past its edge; you stop about 0.45 away).
- Bag cheat fixed: pieces already flying toward you count against the bag,
  so an extractor (or anything else) can never overfill it.
- Rocket: repairing it no longer launches it. Walk up to the repaired rocket
  and a Launch button appears; stay as long as you like and launch later.
  After lift-off, "Keep exploring" brings you back and the rocket waits again.

Bigger planet, 100% reachable
- Planet radius 93 (was 62, +50%). 21 bubble generators on a hex-like
  layout; every generator has 4 sizes (9 / 15 / 22 / 30). With all of them
  maxed the whole planet comes alive (checked: 100%).
- Generator costs come from a formula by distance from home (tier 0-3),
  ready for the planet generator later. The final, biggest size of every
  non-home bubble needs polished crystal, so 100% needs refining.
- Launch still unlocks at 50%. Reaching 100% earns the gold planet badge
  (gold bar and star in the HUD, shown on the lift-off screen). The badge
  is the hook for the carry-over bonus on the next planet (to design).
- Two broken bridges now (west and east). 3 extractors, 6 lost astronauts,
  more trees/rocks/crystals/ore spread over the bigger map.

Refining (new)
- Three broken machines to repair, then load, wait, collect (walk past):
  Kiln:      2 stone + 2 red crystal -> polished crystal (6s)
  Smelter:   2 gold + 1 wood         -> gold bar (7s)
  Assembler: 1 polished crystal + 1 gold bar -> power core (10s, tier 3;
             its repair needs refined items)
- Each machine's sign shows its recipe, a "Load" row for hopper space,
  stored output and progress.
- Rocket now needs: 30 stone, 4 polished crystal, 4 gold bars, 3 power
  cores. The golden pickaxe needs gold bars.
- Saves are v2 (the bigger planet starts fresh once).

Playtest (automated): touch gathering, extractor bag limit, kiln repair /
load / collect, both bridges, 100% coverage, gold badge, launch button.

PHASE 3 — touch, shed, signposts, crew
- Every transfer is touch-only now: you have to stand on a thing's pad or
  sign to give it materials or take from it.
- Refineries are long machines: blue IN pad (load, shows loaded/max) at one
  end, green OUT pad (pickup, shows Ready n/max) at the other. Load and
  storage are both 10 (Assembler 6). The label now matches what it takes.
- Storage shed (next to home): starts broken. Repair it, then stand on its
  yellow pad and tap a material in the left panel to store all of it, or tap
  again to take some back. Level 1 holds 100 raw; Level 2 holds 200 and adds
  polished crystal + gold bars; Level 3 holds 350 and adds power cores.
- Workbench is option C: four signposts (pickaxe, axe, backpack, suit).
  Walk onto one to pay toward that upgrade; partial payments are kept.
- Rescued astronauts get jobs by rescue order: Ava wood, Kai stone, Mira
  explorer, Leo red crystal, Juno gold, Theo explorer. Gatherers chop/mine
  on their own side of the river and drop it in the shed. Explorers search
  dead spots and bring back random finds (sometimes refined items).
  Crew wait by the shed until it's repaired.
- Not yet: refinery upgrades for bigger batches.

PHASE 4 — storage limits, tiers, crystal bridge, upgrades, map, boarding
- Storage: every item has its own limit in the shed: raw 50 each, refined 25
  each (polished crystal, gold bars), manufactured 10 each (power cores).
  Shed levels now unlock stages instead of total space: LVL 1 raw, LVL 2 adds
  refined, LVL 3 adds manufactured. A 4th stage (5 each) is reserved for a
  second manufacturing step on later planets.
- Crew: a gatherer whose material is full in the shed goes exploring
  (label shows "exploring") and returns to gathering once there's room.
  Explorer finds only include raw items the shed has room for; refined
  finds ~30% (45% when all raw shelves are full), manufactured ~5% (9%).
- Bridges: the first is wood and stone. The second is now a glowing crystal
  bridge built from 36 red crystal only. Planet generator will pick the
  second bridge's material (crystal or gold) at random on later planets.
- Bubble generators all follow one pattern: LVL 1 (build) raw, LVL 2 more
  raw, LVL 3 refined (polished crystal + gold bars), LVL 4 MAX power cores.
  Counts grow with distance from home: 1+1 refined and 1 core near home,
  up to 2+2 refined and 2 cores at the far edge. All 21 maxed = 56 refined
  + 25 power cores.
- Refinery upgrades: a sign in front of each machine. LVL 2 and LVL 3 (MAX)
  work faster (0.7x, then 0.5x the time) and hold 1.5x / 2x the loads and
  tray. The cap turns gold at MAX.
- Map: round button top-left (planet with the river). Opening it pauses
  everything and blurs the world. Only discovered ground shows: green alive,
  grey dead, river blue (water) or orange (lava). Markers, red until MAX or
  fixed, green when done: circle bubble, square machine (refineries,
  extractors, shed, workbench), bar bridge, triangle rocket. Blue dot is you.
  Lost astronauts never show. "Explored %" and "Alive %" underneath.
  Discovery: about a screen's worth around you as you walk, a circle of 24
  around the start is known from the beginning, and explorers chart the map
  as they wander. Saved with the game.
- Launch: a 10-second countdown above the rocket. Your crew walk in (far
  ones appear from just off screen) and disappear behind the rocket through
  a hatch you never see; you board last. Liftoff at zero.

PHASE 4.1 — shed tap fix
- Bug: with some of a material in your bag and a full shelf, tapping did
  nothing forever (it only took items out when your bag had none).
- Now one tap stores if any of it can go in; if nothing can (shelf full, or
  the shed isn't upgraded for it), the tap takes some out instead, up to
