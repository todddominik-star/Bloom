THE IDEA (Todd)
---------------
A calm space adventure inspired by Life Bubble. An astronaut is stranded on
a dead little planet. A glowing bubble keeps them alive; growing the bubble
brings the planet back to life (cracked red earth turns to grass, the lava
river runs blue, bare trees turn green). Go places, gather, collect, earn
better tools, and get better at things over time. No enemies, no losing.

Todd's long-term design
- Planets after the first come from a random generator (same screen,
  structures and obstacles chosen at random) instead of hand-made layouts.
- Each planet uses 3-6 materials from a long master list that grows.
- One or two materials upgrade a machine or rebuild a broken pre-placed
  item (like the bridge). Find and collect enough to restore the planet.
- Bubble generators upgrade a few times; sometimes a bubble must be maxed
  out to connect to the next area. Maxed equipment shows MAX instead of costs.
- Oxygen: an air timer runs outside the bubbles (rescue astronauts, mine
  just outside, cross dead ground as a shortcut). Suit upgrades add time.
- Easter eggs (later): an obscure spot that, mined or cleared enough,
  reveals a small cave / room / old building / old ship with special
  non-critical materials that make the planet faster or cheaper to finish.
- Carry-over item (later): one special item taken to the next world and
  combined there with a few local resources makes a tool 3-5 levels better.
- Level up: skills grow with use; tool upgrades; collection album with set
  rewards (later); rescued astronauts as helpers; upgrade machines in play.

PHASE 1 (built)
---------------
Engine and look
- three.js, smooth rounded toy-like shapes, bright colours, close angled
  camera, press-and-pull joystick (and arrow keys / WASD on a laptop).
- The planet is a floating disc (radius 62) over a starfield.
- Ground shader: cracked red earth (Voronoi cracks) outside bubbles, grass
  inside, a cyan glow at every bubble edge. Up to 12 bubbles.
- River ribbon: lava outside a bubble, flowing water inside.
- Grass tufts and flowers grow only inside bubbles; pebbles only outside.
  Trees switch between bare/dead and green as the bubble reaches them.
- Bubble domes: soft cyan fresnel shells that grow smoothly.
- Auto quality: lower resolution, then no shadows, if the phone struggles.

Planet 1 "Ember" (src/planet.js is a description; a generator will
produce the same shape later)
- Materials: wood (trees), stone (boulders), red crystal, gold (ore; needs
  the crystal pickaxe).
- 7 bubble generators (home is built; 6 build sites with signposts),
  each with 2-4 levels and per-level costs, MAX badge when done.
- Broken bridge across the river (repair to reach the north).
- 2 extractors (red crystal, gold): build, then they mine on their own into
  storage; walk past to collect; upgrade for speed/storage.
- Workbench (inside the starting bubble): pickaxe, axe, backpack, suit.
- 4 lost astronauts in HELP bubbles; walk up and lead them into any bubble.
  Each rescue: +5 bag space; they wait by the workbench and wave.
- Air: 12s outside at first (20/30/45 with suit upgrades). At zero, the
  suit floats you gently back into the nearest bubble; nothing is lost.
- Skills: mining and woodcutting levels rise with use (5% faster swings per
  level, and a growing chance of a bonus piece).
- Goal: 50% of the planet alive, then repair the rocket -> lift off.
  (All generators maxed gives about 57%.)
- Autosave every 5s and when the app is hidden; Continue / Start over.

Playtest (automated): gathering, generator upgrade, building outside with
air, float home, bridge repair, rescue, workbench upgrade, extractor, rocket
and lift-off all worked; no errors.
